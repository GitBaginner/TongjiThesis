\MakeAbstract{
    本文针对基于自然语言的机械臂多任务通用控制方法展开系统性研究。考虑到以自然语言为交互接口降低机械臂使用门槛、拓展应用场景等实际需求，本研究针对当前机械臂控制中多任务泛化能力不足以及缺乏自然语言到底层控制指令的跨模态映射等问题，提出了从强化学习专家策略训练到轻量级语言模型指令微调的完整技术路线。本文包含的主要工作内容如下：

    （1）针对高质量专家轨迹数据的获取问题，提出了基于TQC算法与HER机制的机械臂单任务专家策略训练方案。该方法针对Fetch机械臂的Reach、Push、Slide与PickAndPlace四项基础操作任务，利用TQC算法结合HER机制在MuJoCo仿真环境中训练独立的单任务专家策略；同时，通过消融实验对比仅TQC与TQC+HER的训练表现，分析HER在不同任务复杂度下的作用。实验结果表明，所训练的专家策略在四项任务上的评估成功率分别达到100.0\%、97.9\%、99.0\%与90.6\%。实验验证HER在稀疏奖励环境中能有效加速训练收敛，但在部分任务中反而会降低策略网络的最终性能。

    （2）针对物理状态与自然语言之间的跨模态数据转化问题，设计了从物理观测到结构化文本的异构数据转化流程与基于闭环回放的轨迹清洗方法。该流程首先对专家策略输出的数值观测轨迹进行语义切分与离散化，将其映射为具有物理含义的结构化自然语言字段；进而通过三角色对话模板封装，转化为语言模型可处理的文本格式；最终提出基于物理引擎闭环回放的轨迹采集与清洗方法，对转化后的数据进行可复现性验证与清洗。实验结果表明，四项任务经闭环回放验证后的轨迹保留率分别为100.0\%、90.6\%、92.6\%与70.0\%，剔除不可复现的轨迹后，数据集内所有轨迹均通过物理引擎回放验证。

    （3）针对轻量级语言模型在多任务控制场景下的跨模态动作生成问题，利用构建的数据集在6个0.6B至1.3B参数的轻量级基座模型上开展了LoRA微调实验。该方法首先在单任务设定下分别评估各模型的动作预测精度；而后开展多任务混合微调实验，借助vLLM推理引擎实现闭环推理与环境测试，分析跨任务数据混合对不同性能水平模型的影响。实验结果表明，Qwen3.5-0.8B以54.9\%的平均成功率取得最优性能，Gemma3-1B与Llama3.2-1B分别获得16.5与12.85个百分点的多任务增益，说明跨任务知识迁移对多数模型具有正向增益，所有模型的推理延迟均在1\textasciitilde10 Hz范围内，具备实时控制的潜力。

}{机械臂控制，大语言模型，参数高效微调，强化学习，行为克隆}

\MakeAbstractEng{
This paper presents a systematic study on multi-task universal control methods for robotic arms based on lightweight large language models. Considering the practical needs of lowering operational barriers and broadening application scenarios through natural language interaction, this research addresses the problems of insufficient multi-task generalization and the lack of cross-modal mapping from high-level natural language to low-level control commands in current robotic arm control, and proposes a complete technical pipeline from reinforcement learning expert policy training to lightweight language model instruction fine-tuning. The main contributions are as follows:

(1) To address the acquisition of high-quality expert trajectory data, a single-task expert policy training scheme based on the TQC algorithm with HER mechanism is proposed for robotic arm manipulation tasks. The method trains independent single-task expert policies for the Reach, Push, Slide, and PickAndPlace tasks of the Fetch robotic arm in the MuJoCo simulation environment using TQC combined with HER. Meanwhile, ablation experiments are conducted to compare TQC alone versus TQC+HER, analyzing the effect of HER under different task complexities. Experimental results show that the trained expert policies achieve evaluation success rates of 100.0\%, 97.9\%, 99.0\%, and 90.6\% on the four tasks, respectively, and verify that HER effectively accelerates training convergence in sparse reward environments but may reduce the final performance of policy networks in certain tasks.

(2) To address the cross-modal data transformation between physical states and natural language, a heterogeneous data transformation pipeline from physical observations to structured text and a trajectory cleaning method based on closed-loop replay are designed. The pipeline first performs semantic segmentation and discretization on the numerical observation trajectories output by expert policies, mapping them into structured natural language fields with physical meanings. Then, three-role dialogue templates are applied to convert the data into text formats processable by language models. Finally, a trajectory collection and cleaning method based on physics engine closed-loop replay is proposed to verify and clean the transformed data for reproducibility. Experimental results show that the trajectory retention rates after closed-loop replay verification are 100.0\%, 90.6\%, 92.6\%, and 70.0\% for the four tasks. After removing non-reproducible trajectories, all remaining trajectories in the dataset pass physics engine replay verification.

(3) To address the cross-modal action generation of lightweight language models in multi-task control scenarios, LoRA fine-tuning experiments are conducted on six lightweight base models ranging from 0.6B to 1.3B parameters using the constructed dataset. The method first evaluates the action prediction accuracy of each model under single-task settings; then conducts multi-task mixed fine-tuning experiments with closed-loop inference and environment testing via the vLLM inference engine, analyzing the impact of cross-task data mixing on models of different performance levels. Experimental results show that Qwen3.5-0.8B achieves the best average success rate of 54.9\%, while Gemma3-1B and Llama3.2-1B gain 16.5 and 12.85 percentage points of multi-task improvement respectively, indicating positive cross-task knowledge transfer for most models. The inference latency of all models falls within the 1\textasciitilde10 Hz range, demonstrating the potential for real-time control.

}{Robotic arm control, Large language models, Parameter-efficient fine-tuning, Reinforcement learning, Behavior cloning}
